Device sessions, the browser's part. A session the server ended (`401 session_revoked`: signed out from another device, rejected, refresh-token reuse) is final at once: renewal stops without retrying or asking `/auth/me`, and `onSessionLost` runs once with reason `"revoked"` (`"expired"` when renewal is simply impossible). The browser singleton dispatches a cancelable `SIGNED_OUT_EVENT` (`redsuite:signed-out`, `detail: { reason }`) on `window` before it goes to /login, so a shell can show its own signed-out screen. `401 session_upgrade_required` (a badge from before device sessions) renews once, even right after another renewal, and replays; `bootstrapAuthUser` does the same at load, with or without a renewal stamp. `createWebSocket` never reconnects after a `4401` close (`SESSION_REVOKED_CLOSE_CODE`) and calls `onSessionRevoked`; the WebSocket provider reports it through `reportSessionRevoked()`. `AuthUser.device` (`{ sessionId, status }`) is what `/auth/me` now reports.
