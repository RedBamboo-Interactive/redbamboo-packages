Dock lanes: a side zone can hold several columns side by side. The layout model is v2 (zone, lanes, groups, tabs; each lane has its own pixel width) and a saved v1 layout migrates without loss, each zone becoming one lane. Panels declare an `affinity` (`"list"` for the MasterDetailLayout list, `"tools"` by default): a panel with no saved placement joins the innermost column of its kind, or gets its own column beside the centre (tools) or at the screen edge (the list), so moving the list to the right and opening Settings gives centre | Settings | list. New drop targets on a group's inner and outer edges and on a zone's outer edge start a new column, the panel menu gains "Move to own column", and `useDockController` exposes `moveToOwnColumn` and `canMoveToOwnColumn`. When columns and the 640px centre floor (`DOCK_CENTER_FLOOR`) do not fit, a side renders its columns stacked in one, without changing the saved layout or remounting anything. A v1 lane that stacked the list with tool panels (the list docked right, then Settings opened) is split once, when every panel in it has registered: tool groups move to a column beside the centre, the list keeps its column; tabs mixing list and tools, and stacks built in v2, are left alone.
